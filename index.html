FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Financing FAQs

Will pre-qualifying affect my credit score?

No. Our pre-qualification process utilizes a soft credit inquiry that does not impact your credit score or appear on your hard credit report.

Can I apply my trade-in toward the down payment?

Absolutely. Your trade-in equity can be directly applied to lower your total financed amount and reduce your monthly payment.

What lenders does Northstar Motors work with?

We work with premier national lenders including Capital One Auto, Chase Auto, Ally Financial, local credit unions, and specialized luxury auto lenders.
